## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Methodology

Every product in the Tech Vartual Tutor catalog was cross-checked against 51 verified Bangladesh marketplace quotations, authorised retail listings, and local electronics distributors (including Daraz Bangladesh, Star Tech, Ryans, Apple Gadgets BD, Techland, Rokomari, Foodpanda Shops, BDSailor, BDStall, and Ofuronto).

**Pricing Governance Hierarchy:**
1. **Exact-Model Quotes:** Verified retail listings for identical manufacturer brand and model.
2. **Comparable-Model Quotes:** Benchmark pricing from identical hardware batches or closest known models when exact brand labels differ.
3. **Provisional Estimates:** Identified products requiring supplier verification before final retail pricing is published.

## 5. Pricing Governance Recommendations

1. **Consistent Bangladeshi Taka Formatting:** All prices on customer-facing screens must use Bengali/English standard taka formatting (`৳599`, `৳1,290`, `৳2,490`).
2. **Real vs Inflated Strike-Through Prices:** Strike-through original prices (`originalPrice`) must never be artificially inflated. They reflect documented authentic market benchmarks.
3. **Provisional Price Safeguard:** For products where exact hardware batches remain unverified with the supplier (e.g. unread package batches from shelf stock), mark prices as provisional until physical warehouse testing is completed.
4. **Delivery Fee Integration:** Orders of ৳2,000 or above automatically qualify for Free Delivery across Bangladesh (regular delivery: ৳60 inside Dhaka, ৳120 outside Dhaka).
